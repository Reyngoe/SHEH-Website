Our Story

Two Friends, One Code

San Diego, California

The Foundation

Since middle school

We're Jacob and Patrick. We've been best friends since middle school — not the kind who drift apart and reconnect, the kind who never left. High school, the young-adult years, jobs, all of it. We've hiked Mt. Woodson more times than we can count, fished half of San Diego County, and spent over a year sweating on the same hard-labor event crew. Twenty-some years in, we're still showing up for each other. That's the foundation this company stands on.

Who We Are

Built between shifts

Jacob

Family man first — three kids, and nothing ranks above being a good father to them. After that, he's wired the way this whole brand is wired: learn the skill, do the work, build something that lasts. He works long, hard days — the kind of schedule that costs you something — because that's what building a life actually takes. Life has a way of teaching you how to get things done right, if you're paying attention, and he's always working on something, always learning the next thing. Not every project turns into an empire, and that was never the point. The point is to keep learning, keep building things that work, and keep showing up.

Patrick

A serviceman now, but he earned his way there the long way. He started out working for the man like everyone else and never stopped pushing — better skills, better jobs, every step earned. That climb took him into government work in San Diego, and eventually into the U.S. Navy, where he serves today. He's got bigger goals still ahead of him. Bet on him to reach them. And ask the kids in his life who shows up for them — they'll tell you.

2023

It started with a simple problem

We've always been early risers — full-time jobs, brutal hours, one of us up at three in the morning, the other coming off graveyard. The workouts got squeezed into the late afternoons, meeting up to put in the work before getting back to the grind. We were chasing cleaner fuel for all of it and couldn't find it — not on the go, not on the shelf, not without a mile-long ingredient list and junk sweeteners to match. We wanted something simple: a clean bar you could take anywhere and trust. So we made our own. Then came the thought that built the company: other people are looking for this too. It's never gotten more complicated than that. Keep it simple. Keep it clean. Make it work.

No Factory

Two kitchens, the hard way

There's no production line. Batches happen in our kitchens, back and forth — some weeks at his place, some at mine, working out before or after and pressing bars between shifts and after bedtime. Rebeca's in it too, making batches at the house and tasting every round straight. We'd bring them to Patrick, talk them over, and go again.

We've pressed bars that came out right and bars that absolutely didn't. Ratio experiments gone wrong. A chocolate peanut butter dry enough to sand wood. We ate the failures, wrote down what we learned, and went again. And yes — there have been some failed lunges after a long batch night. Quality control has a price.

That's still where we are today. Same two kitchens, same back-and-forth. By hand, no shortcuts, none of it handed to us — and that's the point. We don't need it fast. We need it good. The fight goes on, and this bar is coming. We're building it good, or we're not building it at all.

Our People

The people behind us

Behind these two friends is a whole circle of people who make the work possible. For Jacob, that's Rebeca — holding down the house and the schedule through all the chaos, and getting right in it when the batches are going. For Patrick, it's family and friends who've always had his back. The people who believe in you and let you chase the hard thing — we don't take a second of it for granted. Built not given, but never built alone.
