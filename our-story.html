Our Story

Two Friends, One Standard

San Diego, California

The Foundation

Since middle school

We're Jacob and Patrick. We met in middle school, and we've done just about everything together since — workouts, jobs, the long stretch of years where life gets heavy and you find out who's still standing next to you.

Decades later, the friendship holds. That's the foundation this company is built on.

Who We Are

Built between shifts

Jacob

Ten-plus years in manufacturing and operations — production supervision, team training and management, process control, inventory, scheduling. He also owns and operates a private storage rental business in Riverside County.

A father of three. Known for showing up — logging serious miles every week between work, family, and business commitments.

Patrick

Worked his way up from casino service jobs through civilian manufacturing, then a federal contracting role at a Department of Defense fulfillment center in San Diego. Today he serves in the U.S. Navy, training in electrical engineering and cybersecurity.

Not a father yet — but ask his many nieces and nephews who shows up for them.

2023

It started with a simple problem

We were getting up early, training hard, getting stronger — and we couldn't find a protein bar we actually trusted to fuel it. So we started making our own.

Then came the thought that built the company: other people need this too.

The idea has never gotten more complicated than it was that day. Keep it simple. Keep it clean. Make it work.

No Factory

Two kitchens, the hard way

There's no production line. Batches happen in our kitchens — his one week, mine the next, between shifts and after bedtime.

We've pressed bars that came out right and bars that absolutely didn't. Ratio experiments gone wrong. A chocolate peanut butter dry enough to sand wood. We ate the failures, wrote down what we learned, and went again.

And yes — there have been some failed lunges after a long batch night. Quality control has a price.

The Standard

Wholesome and legit

No investors. No shortcuts. Nothing we wouldn't hand to a friend at the gym. We're building SHEH the same way we'd tell you to build anything: do the right hard thing, keep your word, never give up.

“Start hard today, be harder than yesterday, for today is yesterday’s tomorrow.”
