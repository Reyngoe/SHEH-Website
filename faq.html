Straight Answers

FAQ

No fine print, no runaround. What we know, you know. What we don't know yet, we say so.

What does SHEH mean?

Start Hard, End Hard. Life is hard — early alarms, kids, work, responsibility. We make fuel for the men who meet it.

How much protein? What are the macros?

Straight answer: the final numbers come with the final recipe, and we won't print a number we haven't earned. The engine is whey protein — we're testing isolate to get the most protein per bar. Full macros publish at launch.

What's NOT in the bars?

Zero preservatives. Zero artificial flavors. And never artificial sweeteners. We're building them to be shelf-stable without cutting those corners. The full ingredient list goes public at launch.

Do they contain nuts?

Yes. The current lineup is built on peanuts and almonds. If you have a nut allergy, these bars are not for you.

Are they vegan, gluten-free, or keto?

No. These bars aren't built around special diets — they're built to fuel. Whey protein means dairy, so they're not vegan and won't be.

How do I get one today?

Through us, directly: join the email list at shehthrive.com, or DM @the1sheh on Instagram. When the bars launch, it'll be through us or this website only.

How much will they cost?

Not set yet. Price gets announced at launch, and the email list hears everything first.

When do they launch?

When they're ready. No date yet — we won't rush the standard to hit a deadline.

Will you ship, or is it local only?

The plan is both: online orders and local San Diego pickup, as far as we can afford to reach the people who want them.

Who makes these?

Jacob and Patrick — two friends since middle school, full-time jobs, small batches, two kitchens, San Diego. Read the whole story.

Can I suggest a flavor?

Absolutely. DM @the1sheh. The lineup isn't final, and we listen.

I run a gym or a shop — can I stock SHEH?

When the bars land, we want them where hard work happens. Email the1sheh@gmail.com and we'll talk.

Didn't find your answer? Talk to Roy in the corner, or email the1sheh@gmail.com.
