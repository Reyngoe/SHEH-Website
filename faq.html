Straight Answers

FAQ

No fine print, no runaround. What we know, you know. What we don't know yet, we say so.

What does SHEH mean?

Start Hard, End Hard. It's a way of meeting life: give it your whole effort, finish what you start, and don't quit on yourself in between. The goods carry the name. The code is the rest of it.

What do you sell?

Durable goods for people who live hard and active — gear built to be used rough and last long. Patches come first; shirts, hats, and socks are in the works. Nothing's on the shelf yet, and early descriptions may shift before things land. When something's real, you'll see it here first — and if you're on the list, you'll hear the moment it lands.

Can I buy anything today?

Today, you can't — and we won't pretend you can. Nothing's launched yet. But you can get in line right now: join the email list at shehthrive.com or DM @the1sheh on Instagram. When goods land, the list hears first — and you'll only ever buy through us or this site.

When does the first drop land?

When it's ready. No date yet — we won't rush the standard to hit a deadline. But we won't leave you guessing either: we'll say so as things move, and the list hears the moment it's real.

How much will things cost?

Not set yet. What we can promise: the price will be fair. Prices post when goods land, and the list hears first.

Will you ship, or is it local only?

Small and steady, like everything else we build. The first goods move person to person — hand to hand, word of mouth. Shipping comes as we grow into it, and the list will know the moment it's sorted.

Who's behind SHEH?

Two friends — Jacob and Patrick. Best friends since middle school, working full-time, building this by hand between shifts. The full story's worth a read.

Who's Old Man Roy?

The mentor in the corner. Roy is ours — our character, our mentor, the code with a voice. Every word he says, we wrote. Need a straight word? He's at the bar.

Are the designs your own?

Every design comes through us. Family and close friends throw inspiration in the pot, but nothing wears the mark unless the two of us sign off — we only sell what we'd call good enough for a SHEH.

What about the bars?

The bars are where SHEH started, and they're still on the road — the long road. Nothing's happening with them right now, and we won't pretend otherwise. When that changes, the list hears first.

I run a gym or a shop — can I stock SHEH?

That's the long-term goal. When we're ready to stock beyond direct sales, we want SHEH where hard work happens. Email the1sheh@gmail.com and we'll talk when the time comes.

Can I suggest something?

Absolutely. DM @the1sheh with anything — what you'd want to see, what we could do better. We actually read it all.

Didn't find your answer? Talk to Roy in the corner, or email the1sheh@gmail.com.
