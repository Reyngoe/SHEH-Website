Every other page was fine (they still define the old names).
- FIX: defined the full palette set the widget needs on the homepage :root, aliased to the umber palette (--charcoal-deep + --charcoal kept as two distinct depths so the panel keeps its layered look). The fix lives in the HOMEPAGE, not in mentor.js (mentor.js untouched). Confirmed working — Roy now renders identically on the homepage to every other page.
- The authoritative list of variables mentor.js depends on (grep mentor.js for `var(--`): --bone, --charcoal, --charcoal-deep, --ember, --ember-dark, --font-body, --font-head, --sand, --sand-light, --slate, --slate-light. BROCHURE PRINT AUDIT + FIX — DONE 2026-07-08 (owner test-printed and the fold/duplex/size were off; full pipeline re-audited from first principles, same branch):
- REAL FILE BUGS FOUND & FIXED: (1) IMPOSITION GEOMETRY — all six panels were equal thirds (33.3333%). A C-fold needs the fold-in flap (the physical panel carrying Get the Word outside / Straight Answers inside) 1/16" NARROWER so it tucks flat: widths are now 3.6875" + 3.6875" + 3.625" = 11" on both faces (outside: flap|back|cover, folds at 3.625"/7.3125"; inside: goods|stock|answers-flap, folds at 3.6875"/7.375" — same two physical creases, mirrored coordinates; verified 11−7.375=3.625 and 11−3.6875=7.3125). Fold ticks are now placed per-face at those exact positions (they were at thirds on both faces — wrong on the widths AND identical on both faces). (2) TICKS UNPRINTABLE — the 0.14" edge ticks sat entirely inside a home printer's ~0.25" unprintable border, so no fold guides ever printed; ticks are now 0.4" hairlines (still outside the content area, which starts 0.55" in). (3) TYPE UNDER FLOOR — FAQ answers were 8.5pt and the .org line 7.5pt; print floor is now: body/answers ≥9.5pt, FAQ questions 10pt, nothing under 8pt anywhere. Six Q&As at the new sizes fit panel 4 with ~2.5" to spare — NO copy cut needed, all copy still verbatim owner-approved. (4) Minor: QR gap widened 0.32"→0.4"; cover motto sized to stay ≥0.25" off the fold even with fallback fonts.
- CONFIRMED PRINT-SETTINGS ISSUES (not file bugs): duplex MUST be "flip on SHORT edge" (long-edge — most printers' default — prints side 2 upside down and backs every panel wrong: the #1 "sides don't agree" cause); scale MUST be 100%/Actual size (any "fit to page" shrink explains "content seemed small" and moves the folds off the ticks). The on-page instructions now lead with the five exact settings and include the "side 2 upside down → long-edge flip" troubleshooting line.
