How to print & fold this brochure

  1. Print: File → Print. Paper: Letter. Orientation: Landscape. Two-sided: ON, "flip on short edge." Scale: 100% / "Actual size." Margins: None (or Minimum).
  2. No duplex printer? Print page 1 alone, put the sheet back in the tray printed-side down with the same top edge leading, then print page 2. Test with one sheet first.
  3. Fold (classic C-fold / letter fold): Lay the sheet with the INSIDE spread facing up — that's the side with The Goods / The Stock / Straight Answers. Fold the right panel (Straight Answers) in over the middle, then fold the left panel (The Goods) over the top. The S.H.E.H cover ends up on the front. Faint gray tick marks at the page edges show where the folds go.
