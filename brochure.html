How to print & fold this brochure

Note: the bar lineup shown is preliminary — a small disclaimer appears under the bar list. Get the founder's OK on the disclaimer placement before printing copies.

  1. Print: File → Print. Paper: Letter. Orientation: Landscape. Two-sided: ON, "flip on short edge." Scale: 100% / "Actual size." Margins: None (or Minimum).
  2. No duplex printer? Print page 1 alone, put the sheet back in the tray printed-side down with the same top edge leading, then print page 2. Test with one sheet first.
  3. Fold (classic C-fold / letter fold): Lay the sheet with the INSIDE spread facing up — that's the side with The Bars / Our Story / Straight Answers. Fold the right panel (Straight Answers) in over the middle, then fold the left panel (The Bars) over the top. The S.H.E.H cover ends up on the front. Faint gray tick marks at the page edges show where the folds go.
